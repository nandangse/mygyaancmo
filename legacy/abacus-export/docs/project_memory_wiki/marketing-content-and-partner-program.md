- Marketing Plan: tenant module (nav after Onboarding), one living document combining Positioning & Offer, 1-Page Marketing Plan (9 squares) and Business Model Canvas (9 blocks); AI can draft from company brain + products, team edits in place. Stored in a MarketingPlan model (one per company).
- Book Summaries: public list at /books and one SEO page per book at /books/[slug] (cover, summary, ideas worth exploring, further resources such as YouTube/author site); published books feed the Resources grid on the home page and the newsletter.
- Books CMS: admin screen /admin/books to add, edit, publish/unpublish and delete books (BookSummary model). 10 core B2B marketing books seeded.
- Partner page: /partner is the agency pitch, reached from a slim top banner across the public site ("Are you a digital marketing agency? Partner with us") and a header nav link.
- Agency pitch points: pains (many clients, too many tools, staff/interns taking client data, no consistency); platform and support; special pricing; co-branded portal; structured workflows; super upgrade only for premium customers; certified Mygyaan.ai agency with the 10-hour digital marketing course; use the platform to pitch new customers; Gold and Platinum tiers use the CRM to create offers.
- Agency billing: migrate billing to mygyaan.ai so agency fees are built in; Mygyaan is always prepaid so agencies are paid in advance; payment collection from clients is automated, no chasing.
- Public /testimonials page exists, linked from landing header/footer and partner header; deployed 11-Oct-2026.
- Book Summaries now 22 books (12 added: order 10-21), deployed live.
