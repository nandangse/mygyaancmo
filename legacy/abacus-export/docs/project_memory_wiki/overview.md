- Project: SaaS product at cmo.mygyaan.ai used for mailing and for working with other APIs and tools; a CMO portal for mygyaan.
- Planned scale: 10 companies (tenants), each with an insulated contact list of more than 100,000 contacts and 10 users each.
- Dr Raghu is the member driving the project; Abacus AI has been chosen for building and hosting it (moved from the team still considering Abacus AI).
- First tenant: Nandan GSE, at cmo.mygyaan.ai/Nandan; the portal is modelled on growsalt.com and youngcaesar.digital.
- Goal: a single source of truth for all marketing at a tenant across LinkedIn, Email, WhatsApp, Calls, SEO and SEM. See Product Scope for details.
