---
name: "Tenant Modules: Competition, Knowledge, KPIs, Tools and Rule of One"
description: "Tenant-facing modules beyond channels: competition, company knowledge dump, KPIs.\nTools menu and the Rule of One positioning wizard."
---

- Competition module, organised by segment. Competitor record: Name, Website, Documents (to learn from), Clients, Products, Facility. For Clients, Products and Facility there are two learnings: what we learn (positive) and what we learn but should not follow (negative). AI drafts; a human confirms.
- Company knowledge dump: every person can add company knowledge (members see their own entries, admin sees all raw entries); it feeds the company brain, which is Mygyaan AI use only (see Decisions).
- KPIs: the platform ships default KPIs visible on login. Each tenant can add KPIs and delete them (delete = soft-hide). A tenant-added KPI can later be propagated to other tenants.
- Tools section (tenant menu): Connect Mail & Import (connect Gmail/Outlook, pull contacts into a separate cleanable list; also linked from Settings > Profile) and Find & Merge Duplicates (contacts and companies; AI shows likely duplicates, user selects master and merges).
- Rule of One module (tenant menu, after Marketing Plan): guided wizard with Prep and Result tabs for positioning validation per tenant. It never rewrites the customer's document; it produces a change list where each item carries a "Why", plus a verified LinkedIn audience pulled from Apollo (real contacts only, never fabricated). Stored in a RuleOfOne model.
