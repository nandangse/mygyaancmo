---
name: "Product Scope"
description: "Core CMO portal scope: MQL-stage portal, channels, email workflow and marketing, audits.\nMini CRM option and planned features."
---

- Portal shows all marketing activity up to the MQL (marketing qualified lead) stage; leads can then be pushed to the customer's CRM of choice, or customers can keep working in the portal.
- Channels covered: LinkedIn, Email, WhatsApp, Calls, SEO, SEM.
- Customers can connect their own email IDs; mailing is provider-agnostic (any email API service); email replies must land in the system.
- System must qualify leads and handle unsubscribes.
- Planned features: lookalike domains, lookalike companies, ICP creation.
- The earlier Google Sheet spec was set aside and is to be ignored for planning.
- Email workflow: AI drafts the email from the ICP, user tweaks with one-click AI buttons, can hand-edit and regenerate; AI personalises per recipient for 100 to 200 recipients.
- Website Audit + SEO Audit: 0-100 baseline score, with a week-on-week metrics column. The SEO audit runs automatically every week and surfaces keywords. Work done in Google Sheets for SEO audits and searches must be automated with no manpower.
- Email marketing must let users add campaigns, create mails, and monitor them (recipients, sent, delivered, opens, clicks, replies, bounces). Mail creation lives in Email Studio; a dedicated Email Campaigns module sits under Email Studio (audience = all contacts or a specific list).
- Mini CRM is available for customers without their own CRM; the portal also integrates with the customer's CRM for quote sending.
- SEO Audit page has a Send audit report section: comma-separated emails, remembers and shows the last emailed date. Header cleaned up (shorter ICP text, version badge only in sidebar).
